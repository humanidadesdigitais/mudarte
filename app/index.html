About the project

Mudarte is a light archive for looking through pictures from Brazilian cultural collections and remixing one picture at a time in the browser.

A remix stays in the browser and is not stored on a server.

How it works

  • Choose an institution, then a picture.
  • Draw, erase, and pick a size.
  • Hide, discard, or download the remix.
  • Back or Esc leaves the picture and drops the remix.
